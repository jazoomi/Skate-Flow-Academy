About

Locals deserve to learn without overpaying.

I'm Sadiq — Coach Juice. I've been teaching skating for over a year, and I started Skate Flow Academy because I kept meeting people at the rink who'd paid absurd prices for lessons and still couldn't glide. My focus is footwork — the foundation most coaches skip — up to a waltz jump.

If you've never laced up, or you've been on the ice a few times and still feel shaky, this is for you. I don't currently teach advanced figure-skating elements (axel, twizzle, etc.). Beginners only, done well.

FAQ

Answers to the usual questions.

Where do lessons happen?

Mostly at John Booth Arena and Canlan Ice in North York, near YorkU. I travel for groups elsewhere in Toronto — text to ask.

What gear do I need?

Nothing required. I can provide skates, a helmet, and knee guards if you don't have them. Dress warm and bring socks that fit comfortably inside skates.

What ages and levels do you teach?

Adults, beginner to intermediate — through waltz jump. I don't currently teach advanced figure-skating elements like the axel or twizzle.

How do I book?

Text or call 437-333-2367, or DM @skateflowacademy. I'll send you available times.

How do I pay?

Cash, e-transfer, or WeChat. The 10-pack is prepaid; single sessions can be paid after.

What's the cancellation policy?

Free reschedule with 24 hours notice. Same-day cancellations are charged at 50%.
