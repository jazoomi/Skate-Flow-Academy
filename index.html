About

Edges before everything else.

I'm Sadiq — Coach Juice. I've been teaching skating for over a year, and I built Skate Flow Academy around one conviction: every skater should master edges before anything else. Inside edge, outside edge, clean transitions — the quiet work that makes a stride look effortless.

Most beginner lessons skip straight to gliding and crossovers, which is why so many adults plateau early. We don't. If you've never laced up, or you've been on the ice a few times and still feel shaky on your blades, this is for you. Beginners through the waltz jump — no advanced figure-skating elements (axel, twizzle, etc.).

FAQ

Answers to the usual questions.

Where do lessons happen?

Throughout the Greater Toronto Area. My most common rinks are John Booth Arena and Canlan Ice near YorkU, but I travel across the GTA. A small commute fee may apply for distant locations — text to ask.

What gear do I need?

Nothing fancy. Dress warm and bring socks that fit comfortably inside skates. Limited rental skates are available for a $5 fee. Helmets and knee guards on request.

What ages and levels do you teach?

Adults, beginner to intermediate — through the waltz jump. I don't currently teach advanced figure-skating elements like the axel or twizzle.

How do I book?

Text or call 437-333-2367, or DM @skateflowacademy. I'll send you available times.

How do I pay?

Cash or e-transfer. The 10-pack is prepaid; single sessions can be paid after the lesson.

What's the cancellation policy?

Full 100% refund on cancellation, any time. Reschedule freely — just let me know.
